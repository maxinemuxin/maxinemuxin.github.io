I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by [Chris Callison-Burch](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~ccb/). I am supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP).

I'm originally from Shanghai, China, and I graduated from Harvey Mudd College in 2025. During my undergraduate years, I was very lucky to intern at MIT CSAIL and work with [Jacob Andreas](https://www.mit.edu/~jda/).

My research centers on reasoning in large language models, spanning multimodal reasoning, scientific reasoning, and agentic reasoning.

Outside of research, I'm a pretty active person. You'll usually find me climbing or out on the tennis court. I also love an adventurous road trip, and I'm always on the hunt for new food places to try.

Feel free to reach out with questions or ideas. I'm always happy to collaborate!
